About Lovoni

The Hidden Heart of Ovalau

A Village Like No Other

Hidden within the ancient volcanic crater of Ovalau, Lovoni is one of Fiji's most unique inland villages. Surrounded by lush rainforest and rolling green hills, the village offers visitors a chance to experience traditional Fijian culture in a peaceful natural setting.

Rich History

Lovoni has a fascinating history and strong cultural traditions that have been passed down through generations. Visitors can learn about local customs, legends, and the deep connection between the people and their land.

Why Visit Lovoni?

🌿 Nature

Walk through lush forests and volcanic landscapes.

🏡 Culture

Meet welcoming villagers and experience authentic traditions.

🥾 Adventure

Explore hiking trails, rivers, and scenic viewpoints.
